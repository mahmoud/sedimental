It's been one week since FinFam's launch, and so far so good.

- Show HN post that trended pretty nicely.
- Over 3k unique visitors, which is enough eyeballs to make me quite sure that if someone else had built a collaborative financial planner with an app store for interactive expert opinions, I would have heard about it.
- Great support from friends near and far. Especially gratified from the ones I'm just now meeting through the product. Nothing like it. If you're a new user and we haven't been acquainted yet, let's chat!

Perfect. No regrets. Well, I'm going to be honest. I've got one regret. Product Hunt.

My launch post on LinkedIn mentioned we'd launched on ProductHunt, and I even sent an email out to a few product-oriented friends with a link to our post on ProductHunt.

Hours later I would realize that Product Hunt is, sadly, no more. At least not the Product Hunt I knew from my days on Stripe Invoicing. What's left is a husk, a zombie platform. Active in appearance alone.

## The Indo-European Grift

ProductHunt has a weird quirk where it starts at midnight Pacific time.
Much like with Hacker News, the first few hours of a posting determine its longevity in the cycle.
Due to this scheduling idiosyncracy, something ostensibly from Silicon Valley and largely targeting Silicon Valley is quite unlikely to be determined by people in the states.
Instead, Europe, APAC, and in particular India have an outsized influence in determining the agenda for the day.

## Can Product Hunt be revived? Should it?

I suspect this was always inevitable. When founders exit, etc.

ProductHunt had several design flaws:

- The 24-hour cycle starting at midnight Pacific.

Its greatest strengths as a product have now become major weaknesses:

- Gamification has been gamed

## Goodbye ProductHunt

I suspect we won't see the likes of you again, it was fun while it lasted.

Google Glass Cat is so last decade. Besides, if you're looking for a "hunt" mascot, the obvious choice is a duck.
